# Egypt source book (verified 2026-11-25)

Official / primary sources
- Egyptian Exporters Directory (Ministry of Investment & Foreign Trade): https://www.expoegypt.gov.eg/exporter_directory
  - Company pages include exporter registration number, products, target markets and contacts. Search by HS/product category: https://www.expoegypt.gov.eg/products/category/1
- Egyptian Customs Authority, statistical reports: https://www.customs.gov.eg/Services/Reports
- Ministry of Agriculture, foreign-trade statistics: https://agri.gov.eg/databases/46
- GOEIC Approved Exporter System lists (company name, commodity, HS code, approval code): https://www.goeic.gov.eg/ (search: "Approved Exporter System" PDF)
- General Authority for Investment and Free Zones: https://www.gafi.gov.eg
- CAPMAS: https://www.capmas.gov.eg — Central Bank of Egypt: https://www.cbe.org.eg

Market / trade data
- Trade Map (ITC): https://www.trademap.org — Egypt HS6 export/import flows by destination.
- Commercial aggregators (use with source labels, never as sole evidence): Panjiva, Volza, ImportGenius.

Company-level OSINT
- Company website: About / Products / Certifications / Contact pages; check domain age via WHOIS.
- LinkedIn company + people pages; Hunter.io for email pattern; email verification before sending.
- Google Maps listings for packing house / warehouse scale; Google reviews and complaints.
- Search dorks: `"<company>" site:expoegypt.gov.eg`, `"<company>" GlobalG.A.P.`, `"<company>" export`, `"<company>" complaint OR scam OR lawsuit`.

Certification bodies / registries
- GlobalG.A.P. certified producer lookup: https://www.globalgap.org (Certified Producers database)
- ISO 22000 / HACCP certificates: verify with the issuing certification body, not a website logo.
- Organic: check the control body named on the certificate; Halal: verifying authority and destination-market acceptance.

Query templates
- `fresh produce exporter Egypt site:expoegypt.gov.eg/co/`
- `"<product>" exporter Egypt "GlobalG.A.P."`
- `Egypt <product> exporters GCC buyers LinkedIn`
- `"<company name Arabic>" تصدير خضار`

Caveats
- Official directory data may be stale; always confirm by phone/email.
- A missing directory entry does not prove a company is not a real exporter.
- Export volumes from third-party aggregators are estimates; label as such.
